## §1. What I believe before reading (all to be verified)

- **Depth 2 is known.** Parity (in `P ⊆ E^NP`) needs `2^{Ω(n)}` DNF/CNF size, so a depth-2 case of the lower bound is **unconditional**. *If HLZ26's equivalence is uniform in the depth, the depth-2 instance of (S1) should hold outright*, which would make it an unconditional `FP^NP` algorithm for depth-2 (DNF-output) Avoid at polynomial stretch. **Check whether HLZ26 or earlier work (GLW22, ILW23) already states this.**
- **Depth 3 is the famous wall.** The best explicit bounds are about `2^{Ω(√n)}` (Håstad; Paturi-Pudlák-Zane for parity at depth 3), and **truly exponential depth-3 lower bounds for an explicit function are open**. Valiant's reduction makes them sufficient for superlinear lower bounds on log-depth circuits (`2^{ω(n/log log n)}` at depth 3 suffices). **Whether that openness persists for functions in `E^NP`**, where diagonalization is available, is what (Q1) must settle from a source. My belief: even for `E^NP`, truly exponential depth-3 is open. The Kannan-style diagonalization gives `Σ₂E` (not `E^NP`) against any fixed-depth class of size `2^{εn}`.
- **General depth `d`:** Håstad's `2^{Ω(n^{1/(d−1)})}` for parity. Truly exponential at fixed `d ≥ 3` is open.

## §6. HLZ26's depth accounting forecloses small-depth instances

HLZ26 (extracted copy of ECCC TR25-049), verbatim: the succinct circuit for `y*` has *"total size `O(k · n^{1+ε+c}) = O(n^{1+ε+c})` and depth `(2 + d)k + 2`"*, and *"Assuming `C ∈ AC0_d` ... to ensure that `g ∈ AC0`, we must take `k = O(1)`"*, with the hard function required to need *"`AC0_{dk}` circuits of size at least `ℓ^γ`"*. **Even a depth-1 or depth-2 generator needs a `2^{Ω(n)}` lower bound against AC⁰ of depth `(2 + d)k + 2 ≥ 5`.** My pre-registered depth-2 candidate (parity's unconditional DNF bound) **does not transfer**, and 38a has no instance. The equivalence is stated for the whole class, not per depth.

**Version split, recorded per the practice line.** The extracted copy numbers the Avoid equivalence **Theorem 1.5**, *"For any circuit class C such that AC0 ⊆ C (e.g., AC0, ACC0, TC0, NC1), E^NP requires 2^{Ω(n)} size C circuits if and only if there is an FP^NP algorithm for C-Avoid[n, n^{1+ε}]"*, with the Remote-Point version at 1.6. W3 quoted **Theorem 1.6** without NC¹ and with the footnote that an NC¹ equivalence was withdrawn; TR25-049 has a revision 2. **Same statement, different version; neither page is in error.**

## §7. The re-pricing (Q1, Q3)

- **(i)** The lower-bound side implies **`E^NP ⊄ NC¹`** (W3, quoting Korten's survey §8.1: boosting AC⁰ bounds to `2^{Ω(n)}` for a function in `EXP^NP` *"would imply the breakthrough `EXP^NP ⊈ NC¹`"*, via `NC¹ ⊆ AC0_d[2^{n^δ}]`).
- **(ii)** At every fixed depth `≥ 3` it asks for a **truly exponential** bound. Gurumukhani-Kleber-Paturi-Rosin-Talebanfard (arXiv 2601.04446, 2026): *"the best known depth-3 lower bound for an explicit function is only `2^{Ω(√n)}` and beating this barrier remains a major open problem"*; and *"Valiant showed that any linear-size fan-in 2 circuit of logarithmic depth can be computed by a Σ3 circuit of size `2^{O(n/log log n)}`"*.
- **(iii)** §6: no small-depth unconditional instance.
- *Marked from memory, not re-sourced:* Håstad's `2^{Ω(n^{1/(d−1)})}` for parity at depth `d`.

**(Q2) sweep.** The ECCC keyword page "range avoidance" (keyword/20131) lists TR25-030, 034, 104, 121, 191, 210, 220 and TR26-118 for 2025-2026. **TR25-034** (Kuntewar-Sarma) treats monotone and symmetric NC⁰₃. The other titles were checked by title only. **None settles AC⁰-Avoid at polynomial stretch.**

> **(S1), re-priced:** a truly-exponential constant-depth lower bound for `E^NP`; implies `E^NP ⊄ NC¹`; beyond the `2^{Ω(√n)}` depth-3 barrier; **not smaller than known walls.**
